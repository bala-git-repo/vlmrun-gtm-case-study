Write-up · Part A + Part B

VLM Run GTM build: the write-up

A1–A6 and B1–B4 in a few lines each. The flowchart is B5.

A1 FindA2 EnrichA3 ScoreA4 The hundredA5 SegmentsA6 Scale B1 SignalsB2 PeopleB3 CopyB4 Personalization

Part A · The list

A1Find

  • Started from VLM Run's 4 real customers: small vertical software companies whose product eats messy paperwork.
  • 6 Blitz search recipes, tightened from v1 to v4 by hand-checking samples. Software-only industries and a competitor blocklist did most of the cleanup.
  • Batch 2: lookalikes of our top scorers plus 6 more countries, which raised the #100 score from 42 to 51 with competitors cut.

A2Enrich

  • Free first: 565 → 232 (Blitz about text) → 194 (their own website).
  • Kept for each company: visual core, docs / images / video, a word-for-word quote + URL, and regulated-data and deployment flags.
  • Paid last, survivors only: ML/AI hiring and engineering headcount.

A3Score

  • 100 points, evidence only. 70 for the need (visual core, messy input, video, extracting today) and 30 for the buyer (sensitive data, hiring now, right size).
  • Minus points for building their own vision models, being a services firm, or being acquired.
  • Checked three ways: real customers land #14, #17 and #26 · moving any weight ±20% keeps 93+ of the top 100 · 10 rows checked by hand → 4 rule fixes.

A4The hundred

  • #1 Avallon AI 85 → #100 MoveDocs 51. 73 of 100 score 60+.
  • Competitor check: 27 companies that sell extraction or vision AI themselves were cut, and 17 that built their own OCR or vision models take −15 (e.g. Coral, #28). Every account left reads messy docs, photos or video inside its own product and doesn't sell that as the product.
  • Every row: scores, play, who to contact, a verified quote. In the Sheet, tab Top 100.

A5Segments

  • A · Regulated doc intake (56): lead with accuracy plus zero retention, BAA and VPC.
  • B · Field & freight (32): lead with docs and photos in one API.
  • C · Video-first (12): lead with video into JSON.
  • Leave out anyone who sells extraction or vision AI, and anyone who built their own VLM.

A6Scale

  • ~2,300 companies match our tight searches, and we pulled ~1,000. About 1 in 10 makes the top grade.
  • ~19 credits per top-100 account. Covering the rest costs ≈2,600 credits, so we stopped under our cap.
  • What breaks: keyword splits, docs wrong on cost, fake evidence, stale signals.

Part B · The outreach

B1Signals

  • Company: hiring ML/doc-AI engineers, job posts naming Textract or Tesseract, a new intake product page, VLM Run signups.
  • People: a new CTO or Head of AI, a customer champion who moved, someone posting about OCR pain.
  • A strong signal sends the signal email within 48h. Signals change timing, not targeting.
  • Checked live with Crustdata: 36 of the top 100 have a signal now: 8 are hiring engineers for OCR or document extraction, 19 posted about document pain (e.g. xCures on scanned faxes), Transflo and xCures have a new engineering/AI leader, and Pace, Outmarket, Digs and Lama AI raised recently. The posts also catch fit problems: Coral and iCustoms built their own OCR/IDP, so they take −15. Full table in the sheet tab Crustdata enrichment.

B2People

  • 30 / 40 / 40 titles → Blitz people search → waterfall (decision maker > influencer > champion) → email finders → MillionVerifier → BounceBan for catch-alls → one sendable.csv.
  • No email found → LinkedIn only.
  • Tested on the top 100: Crustdata found tech decision makers or founders at 93 of 100 (163 people, names and LinkedIn only, nobody contacted).

B3Copy

  • 3 emails + a signal version per play. 25–55 words, one fact, an offer to run their samples. See the emails →
  • LinkedIn: founder and growth accounts connect the same day, then wait 30–60 days before one message. Lowest-scored accounts go first.

B4Personalization

  • One line from their own site, rewritten from the saved quote. No quote → segment fallback. Works the same for 1,000 companies.
  • Examples: Inflooens (Claude + GPT-4o-mini for documents) · NikoHealth (fax → pre-filled order) · Zeitro (phone photos of pay stubs) · Alvys (driver BOL photos) · Blitzz (OCR in live video).

1,921 of 10,000 Blitz credits and 194.65 of 199 Crustdata credits spent. Nobody contacted. Full detail sits behind each box in the flowchart.
